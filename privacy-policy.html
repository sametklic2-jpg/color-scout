Summary

Color Scout does NOT require an account and never sends your data to a server. Your game data (progress, best times, streak, badges, settings) lives only on your own device.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, solved rounds, statistics, daily streak, level, badges, theme/language/sound settings and the optional display name you type are stored in your device local storage.

This information never leaves the device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or choose Settings → "Reset all data".

Ads

The app shows ads (Google AdMob). To serve ads, Google may process technical information such as your advertising ID, IP address and approximate location.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland a consent form (UMP/GDPR) appears on first launch; if you decline, non-personalised ads are shown.

Interstitial (full-screen) ads appear only when you start a new free game and only occasionally; no ad is shown while you are playing. No banners and no rewarded ads are used. If you bought the ad-free version you see no ads at all.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play; your payment details never reach us, Google processes the transaction.

The purchase is recorded in your Google Play account, so "Restore purchases" brings your entitlement back after a reinstall.

Notifications

Daily reminder: once you grant notification permission, the app schedules a LOCAL reminder on your device every day at 18:00; nothing is sent from a server. You can decline the permission or turn it off under Android Settings → Apps → Color Scout → Notifications.

Children's privacy

The app targets a general audience. It is NOT directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights and data deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you are fully in control: Settings → "Reset all data" clears game data, and uninstalling removes everything.

You can manage advertising-ID preferences in the Android settings (Ads → Reset/delete advertising ID).

Changes and contact

This policy is updated when needed; significant changes are announced in release notes. Last update: 07.11.2026.
