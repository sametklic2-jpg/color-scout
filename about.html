What is Color Scout?

Color Scout is a calm attention game: find the tile whose colour differs from the others. Exactly one tile is different each round; a wrong tap adds a time penalty, a correct tap moves you on.

How it works

The daily game has 13 rounds and grows from 3×3 to 9×9; each size is played twice and the last round is 9×9. Difficulty starts easy and ends at expert level.

You can also play a free 5-round game in a difficulty of your choice: easy, medium, hard and expert. Rounds are generated on your device.

The daily sequence uses a fixed date-based seed, so everyone plays the same rounds without a server.

Where is my data?

There is no server. All progress is kept on your device and the game is fully playable offline.

Languages and content

The interface is available in 9 languages (Turkish, English, German, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch). Colours are generated on the device instead of using image assets, which keeps the app small.
